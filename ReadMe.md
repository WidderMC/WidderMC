# 👋 Hey, I'm Widder!

I'm **Widder**, a Minecraft developer focused on building **small, lightweight and mostly server-side mods & datapacks**.

I like making things that are **simple to install, easy to use, and don't get in your way**.  
Most of my projects are built around **vanilla-friendly gameplay, server-side mechanics, and small quality-of-life improvements**.

## 📦 Projects#:

 - **[Better Death](<https://modrinth.com/datapack/better-death-by-widder>)** — A Datapack/Mod that bundles dropped items together after death.
- **[SMP-Starter](<https://modrinth.com/datapack/smp-starter>)** — A Datapack/Mod that simplifies the start of a Minecraft server with a small script that opens the world border, changes the game mode, and enables PvP.
- **[KeepInvTag](<https://modrinth.com/datapack/keepinvtag>)** — A simple Mod/Datapack that allows only players with the `keepinv` tag to keep their items after death.
- **[Item Rename](<https://modrinth.com/mod/widderitemrename>)** — A mod that lets you rename your items with custom colors and styles!
- **[EasyItemSign](<https://modrinth.com/mod/easyitemsign>)** — Sign your items with custom text, colors, and formatting using simple commands.
- **[Web Hub](<https://widdermc.github.io/web-hub/>)** — A website for generating commands for [Item Rename](<https://modrinth.com/mod/widderitemrename>) and [EasyItemSign](<https://modrinth.com/mod/easyitemsign>).
- **[Simple Marry](<https://modrinth.com/mod/simplemarry>)** — A simple mod that lets you marry other players.
- **[AntiEnderdragonRespawn](<https://modrinth.com/datapack/antienderdragonrespawn>)** — A simple Datapack/Mod that prevents the Ender Dragon from being respawned.
